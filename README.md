# Instacart Grocery E-Commerce Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using the Instacart grocery e-commerce dataset.

The objective is to identify purchasing patterns, product preferences, reorder behavior, and customer ordering trends to generate actionable business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and order patterns.
- Identify the most frequently purchased products and categories.
- Evaluate product reorder rates.
- Explore frequently purchased product combinations.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## Key Findings

- Observed customers placed an average of 16.59 orders.
- Banana was the most frequently purchased product, with 230,835 purchases.
- Produce was the leading department, with 4,653,754 product records.
- Approximately 59.07% of purchased product records were repeat purchases by the same customers.
- Dairy & Eggs had the highest department-level reorder rate at 67.01%.
- Bag of Organic Bananas and Organic Strawberries were the most frequently purchased product pair, appearing together in 30,830 orders.

## Business Recommendations

- Optimize inventory availability for high-demand products.
- Develop personalized product recommendations.
- Improve customer retention through reorder-based promotions.
- Optimize marketing campaigns based on customer ordering patterns.
- Implement cross-selling strategies using frequently purchased product combinations.
